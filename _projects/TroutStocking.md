A two-pane map of the Pennsylvania Fish & Boat Commission's adult trout stocking
schedule for Allegheny and the ten counties around it: a list of stocked stream
reaches sortable by distance, recency, upcoming stocking or elevation, beside a
map of the actual stream geometry.

The stocking schedule and the stream shapes come from two different PFBC sources,
joined on water and section number. Everything is scraped once and committed, so
the page never calls PFBC at runtime.
